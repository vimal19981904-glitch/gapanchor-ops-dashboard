### Quarter Summary

**What did not go well**

- Enable automated real-time alerts for incoming WhatsApp and Outlook leads to achieve <15 min response time.
- Standardize cloud sandbox environment setup scripts for Manhattan WMS and Kinaxis.
- Establish direct CRM lead qualification workflows to convert processed enquiries into enrolled customers.

# 2. GapAnchor Monthly Operating Model

The monthly operating model is divided into four focused weeks while keeping lead generation and LinkedIn activity running throughout the month.

## Week 1 — Batch / Delivery Focus

**Primary objective:** Start and organize the month's training batches and delivery operations.

Track:
- Batch launch
- Student/client onboarding
- Trainer allocation
- Session schedule
- Sandbox / access readiness
- Recordings and documentation
- Attendance
- Delivery issues
- Client/student feedback
- Pending payments

**Week 1 target:** Batch should be launched and operational.

---

## Week 2 — Batch / Delivery Focus

**Primary objective:** Complete the initial two-week batch/delivery push and stabilize operations.

Track:
- Batch progress
- Attendance
- Training quality
- Hands-on activities
- Support requests
- Payments received
- Trainer performance
- Client/student satisfaction
- Follow-up opportunities

**Week 2 target:** Delivery should be stable and measurable.

---

- New training program

# 15. SWOT Analysis

## Strengths
1. Deep specialized domain expertise in Manhattan WMS, Blue Yonder, Kinaxis, and SAP S/4HANA.
2. Integrated digital operations dashboard with live WhatsApp Cloud API & SQLite lead engine.

1. Expanding Manhattan ProActive WMS corporate consulting offerings.

## Threats
